02 / MAKE TIME FOR YOUR MUSIC Available on Google Play

METRONOME & PRACTICE

AUREUS

A better space
to practise.

For musicians who care about precise rhythm, focused sessions and steady progress. AUREUS brings a flexible metronome and pitch tools into a calm, black-and-gold practice space.

Set time signatures, subdivisions, tuplets, shuffle and beat accents. Choose classic clicks or sampled drums, save presets, use a session timer, and review your practice statistics.

03 / CLASH OF CROWNS In closed testing

Fanorona: Clash of Crowns

Every move can change
the balance of power.

Discover the traditional strategy board game of Madagascar in a campaign of distinctive rivals. Capture by moving towards your opponent or away from them, link captures into powerful sequences, and plan the move they do not see coming.

Begin with Finn Oakley’s guided introduction. Earn beautifully crafted boards and pieces as you work your way towards a royal showdown.
